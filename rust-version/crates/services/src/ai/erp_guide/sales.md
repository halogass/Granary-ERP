# Sales: quotations, proposals, contracts, catalog and pricing

Sales turns a deal into priced, approved documents: quotations (the price), Solution Proposals (the scope and story), and contracts, all priced from the product catalog.

**Quotations** (/sales/quotations)
- Statuses: draft, sent, accepted, signed, rejected, expired, superseded. Manual moves: draft → sent; sent → accepted, rejected or expired; accepted → rejected. "Approved" is never set by hand (the approval engine does it) and "signed" only comes from recording the customer's PO.
- A sent quotation past its valid-until date (plus sales.quotation.expire_grace_days) is marked expired by a daily job; its owner gets one "re-quote or close the deal?" reminder when it ended within sales.quotation.expired_nudge_days (older ones expire quietly).
- Only drafts can be edited (sales:create_quotation). Delete only draft, rejected or expired (sales:edit_quotation).
- **Approval**: the Owner approves a quotation unless every line is a catalog item at list price (a discount within the SKU's volume tier still counts as list). Every discounted line must say its discount type. Above the policy amount the Owner is also pinged on WhatsApp.
- **Send** (sales:send_quotation): passes the approval gate, then sends the QR-verified PDF by email or WhatsApp; the first send starts Nary's follow-up cadence.
- **Signed**: record the customer's PO (number, date or verbal) → signed; recurring lines create subscriptions.
- **Revise**: only a sent, expired or rejected quotation with no invoices or payment schedule; the original becomes superseded and a new draft revision (-N) is made.
- **Invoice from a quotation** (finance:create_invoice): only when sent, accepted or signed; commercial or proforma, in full or by milestone; never more than the quotation total.

**Solution Proposals** (/sales/proposals)
- Statuses: draft, review, approved, sent, superseded. Editable in draft or review.
- Flow: a fit check proposes discovery; discovery fills a requirements sheet on the deal; Nary drafts the proposal (no accuracy claims), its figures and an internal cost sheet; the Owner confirms, which proposes the quotation draft; the Owner approves every proposal; the proposal is sent together with its quotations.
- Cost sheet and rate card need sales:view_costs (changing the rate card: sales:manage_costs).

**Contracts** (/sales/contracts)
- Statuses: draft, sent_for_signing, signed, expired, terminated. Types: sales, service, msa, nda, partner_agreement, vendor_agreement.
- Moving to sent_for_signing or signed needs Owner approval (sales_contract policy); e-sign by the counterparty, 5 business days, extendable to 10. Completing the e-sign marks it signed.

**Catalog and pricing** (/sales/catalog)
- One SKU per sellable item; prices per currency with a valid-from date; volume tiers per SKU.
- A currency's own price row wins; otherwise it is converted from `catalog.conversion_base_currency` at the recorded FX rate, except currencies in `catalog.independent_currencies`, which never convert.
- **Tiers are private**: never applied automatically; a negotiation lever. A discount up to the tier still counts as list price.
- A manual price needs sales:price_manual and a deeper discount needs sales:apply_discount; either makes the quotation need Owner approval.
- Only a superadmin or the executive role edits the catalog; viewing needs sales:view_catalog.
- The chat sees the whole active catalog (SKU, how it is sold, list price) in its prompt; search_catalog gives one item's full detail.

- **Quotation notes from the house template** (4 Nov 2026, Settings → Sales & CRM → Quotation notes templates): every quotation's notes are one numbered list of bold-labelled clauses in the house order, from the template of the lines' product family (the families of the templates in Settings; first match wins; an export currency adds the Incoterm of the template and swaps the Currency and Tax wording; English). To draft notes now: call quotation_notes_template (quotation_id, or items_json + currency) to see the family, the house clauses and the deal clauses with guidance, then pass ONLY the deal clauses to propose_quotation_draft / propose_quotation_update as notes_deal {clause_key: text} (scope, deliverables, client obligations, delivery, pilot credit 50 to 100% by context, payment_milestones for services). The ERP fills validity, currency, payment terms (and the quotation's payment_terms field: products 100% on order or 60/40 from sales.split_payment_min_unit_equivalents, services the down payment then milestones with the final payment on UAT and the handover certificate, invoices due in sales.quotation.notes.invoice_due_days days), hard stop, non-payment (projects and pilots), payment method, bank charges, tax (follows the line taxes), delivery (hardware: sales.quotation.notes.lead_days_min to _max working days), warranty (from delivery), Care from year 2 (sales.quotation.notes.care_pct) and acceptance. A house clause's wording changes only with notes_override (the Owner or sales:apply_discount); some take an added sentence, and optional ones are added with "include" (site_requirements; delay_penalties, the client-caused carve-out, only when a tender imposes penalties). Never an accuracy figure, never a delay penalty, a discount clause only when the Owner or the pricing permission asks. The check warns in the card and in the editor (Fill notes from template, the warnings panel), and the approval refuses a draft whose notes miss a required clause, are not numbered or break those rules (sales.quotation.notes.block_submit); sent or approved quotations are not re-checked. Free `notes` are still accepted, and checked.
- **Segments and pricing rules** (Settings → Sales & CRM → Pricing rules; CRM > segments): leads, deals and catalog items may carry an optional segment (the setting crm.segments lists the allowed values; empty means free text). A quotation line priced below sales.pricing_rules.min_cost_multiple x its catalog cost (custom and service lines against the labour of the deal's cost sheet) blocks the approval while sales.pricing_rules.block_submit is on, unless the Owner gives a pricing override with a reason (set_pricing_override); a missing cost only warns. A quotation with a proposed catalog price, or a SKU in sales.pricing_rules.owner_approval_skus, always goes to the Owner. Without sales:view_costs the check shows no figures.
- **Linking a quotation to a deal without a revision** (5 Nov 2026): a quotation made before its deal existed (sent, approved, accepted, signed, expired, or a draft) is linked to the deal with Link to deal on the quotation page (sales:create_quotation; POST /sales/quotations/{id}/link-deal) or with propose_quotation_link_deal (quotation, deal_id, set_deal_value default true; a card at Ask). Every revision of the quotation moves with it; by default the deal's value and currency become the total of its current revision, and the deal's timeline gets a "Quotation linked" activity with the value before and after (the deal it leaves gets one too). An accepted or signed quotation also gives the deal its offering line and Pilot flag, unless they were set by hand. The customer document never changes: no revision, no new approval, the same PDF, lines, terms and status. The deal must belong to the quotation's customer; a cancelled or superseded quotation is refused; the same deal again changes nothing. propose_quotation_update with only deal_id on a quotation that is no longer an editable draft answers with this card instead of refusing.
