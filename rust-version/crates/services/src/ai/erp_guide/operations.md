# Operations: helpdesk, maintenance, fleet

The operations modules keep customers supported and the company's own equipment and vehicles running.

- **Helpdesk** (/helpdesk/tickets, /helpdesk/teams; public portal /support): tickets new, open, pending, resolved, closed, from the portal, email, WhatsApp or phone. A public ticket gets response and resolution deadlines from its SLA policy; resolving and closing record whether the SLA was met. Permissions helpdesk:view, helpdesk:manage, helpdesk:configure.
- **Maintenance** (/maintenance/equipment, /requests, /work-orders, /plans): any employee with maintenance:request submits a request (draft, submitted, under_review, approved, rejected); approving it (maintenance:manage) creates a work order and puts the equipment under maintenance; completing the work order makes it active again. Plans schedule recurring work.
- **Fleet** (/fleet/vehicles, /assignments, /fuel-logs, /service): vehicles active, maintenance or inactive, with assignments, fuel, service and odometer logs (fleet:view, fleet:manage).
