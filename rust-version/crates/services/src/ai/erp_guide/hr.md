# HR: employees, attendance, leave, payroll, hiring and offers

HR keeps the people side: employees and the org chart, attendance by face, leave, payroll with income tax and social contributions, and hiring from request to offer letter.

- **Employees** (/hr/employees, /hr/org-chart): active, probation, on_leave, terminated, resigned. hr:manage_employees edits, hr:view_all_employees sees everyone. Terminations: pending → confirmed → processed → completed (hr:initiate_termination).
- **Attendance** (/hr/attendance, kiosk page /clock): face clock-in from a personal phone (liveness challenge, key frames, GPS) or a registered kiosk (it matches locally, the server checks again). Records: present, absent, late, half_day, on_leave, holiday, remote. Overrides need hr:manage_attendance. Thresholds are settings (`hr.face_match_distance`, `hr.geo_max_accuracy_m`, `hr.work_start_time`).
- **Leave** (/hr/leave): pending → approved, rejected or cancelled; reviewing needs hr:approve_leave and nobody approves their own.
- **Payroll** (/hr/payroll): runs are draft → approved → paid (or cancelled). Creating a run (hr:manage_compensation) submits it for Owner approval; paying needs a still-valid approval and posts to the ledger. Income tax withholding, the year-end true-up, social contributions, allowances and brackets are settings (Settings → People & payroll).
- **Hiring** (/hr/hiring): a hiring request (hr:request_hire) goes to the Owner; approved → approved, rejected → on hold. Job openings (/hr/job-openings): draft, published, paused, closed, filled.
- **Applicants** (/hr/recruitment): applied, screening, phone_screen, interview_1, interview_2, technical_test, offer_sent, offer_accepted, offer_rejected, hired, rejected, withdrawn (hr:manage_applicants).
- **Offer letters** (/hr/offer-letters): draft → sent (needs Owner approval; the applicant becomes offer_sent) → accepted, declined or countered; can be cancelled from draft, sent or countered. E-signing completes it as accepted.

- **Face check timeouts and attempts** (6 Nov 2026): the liveness check on a phone or the kiosk tablet gives each step its own time (find the face and hold still, do the action, face the camera again; `hr.face.step_timeouts_ms`, default 8 s, 10 s and 5 s), counted from when that step begins, and a phone's challenge stays valid `hr.face.challenge_ttl_seconds` (at least the steps' total plus 15 s for the upload). "Try again" reuses the open camera. Every check is counted (no photo or face data): HR > Attendance > Attempts shows the last 7 days by outcome (done, timeout, aborted), by device (kiosk or personal), by the step reached, and the timeout rate (GET /hr/attendance/face-attempts/summary?days=7, hr:view_attendance).
- **Passive face check first** (6 Nov 2026): when `hr.face.passive.enabled` is on, a phone or the kiosk tablet first flashes `hr.face.passive.flash_count` soft colours (`hr.face.passive.palette`, `hr.face.passive.flash_ms` each, never under 250 ms) and photographs the face after `hr.face.passive.settle_ms`; a live face reflects the colours (`hr.face.passive.flash_min_corr`, `hr.face.passive.flash_min_delta`) and an anti-spoof model scores the face as real (`hr.face.passive.spoof_min_real`). Phones are judged by the server (the colours are picked by the server), the kiosk judges itself and reports the result. When the check is not sure or fails, the blink or head turn follows in the same check. The clock event keeps the method (passive or active), the scores and the fallback reason; phone fallbacks count in Attempts as passive_inconclusive or passive_failed.
