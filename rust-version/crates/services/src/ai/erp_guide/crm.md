# CRM: organisations, contacts, leads, deals, activities, inboxes

The CRM holds who the company sells to and where each opportunity stands: organisations and their contacts, leads (unqualified interest) and deals (qualified opportunities moving through stages), with activities, follow-ups and the shared inboxes attached to them.

- **Records**: organization, contact (belongs to an organisation), lead (source, status, priority, contact/organisation), deal (stage, probability, value, currency, expected close date), activity (call, email, WhatsApp, meeting, task, note, demo, logged on a lead, deal, contact or organisation). Exact allowed values: crm-picklists. Writable fields: crm-fields.
- **Lead → deal**: a lead moves through its statuses (only the allowed moves, see crm-picklists); a qualified lead is promoted to a deal (Nary proposes `lead.promote` as a card). A disqualified lead needs a reason from the disqualify picklist.
- **Deal stages** run in a fixed order with a default probability per stage (setting `crm.funnel.stage_probabilities`). Lost needs at least one loss reason; Won is set automatically when the contract is signed and the down payment is paid (Nary's playbook).
- **Lead sources** are a picklist (`crm.funnel.lead_sources`): web3forms and inline_web3forms (website forms), whatsapp_direct, email, referral, etc. Use the key, never free text.
- **Ownership**: `crm:view_all` sees everything; `crm:view_own` sees only records assigned to the user. Creating and editing need crm:create_contact / edit_contact / create_lead / edit_lead / create_deal / edit_deal; logging needs crm:log_activity.
- **Inboxes**: the CRM shared inbox is hello@ (IMAP sync of Inbox, Sent and read state; replies go out from the CRM mailbox with the sender's email signature). WhatsApp runs through the company phone (view needs crm:view_whatsapp, send needs crm:send_whatsapp). Every message on a chat linked to a contact is logged as an activity automatically. A chat whose number WhatsApp hides (an "…@lid" id) is matched to its contact by the real number when known, else by the id kept as a hidden link; a phone or WhatsApp field never holds a chat id. Deleting CRM inbox mail is for the Owner or a system admin.
- **Follow-ups** (crm/followups): reminders you set or that rules create (no reply, stale deal), delivered by bell, WhatsApp, then email; a weekly digest goes to the Owner. A quotation's reminders close when it is accepted, signed, rejected or expired.
- **Duplicate leads**: a website form or WhatsApp prospect from someone who already has an open lead or deal joins it; two open leads of one person get a "Merge two leads" card (Nary → Approvals) that moves everything to the lead kept.
- **Duplicates** (crm/duplicates): a scan proposes merges; merging moves everything to the kept record.
- **Pages**: /crm/organizations, /crm/contacts, /crm/leads, /crm/deals, /crm/activities, /crm/followups, /crm/duplicates, /crm/email, /crm/whatsapp.

- **Several CRM inboxes** (Settings → Email & WhatsApp → CRM inboxes, 26 Oct 2026): hello@ is the default inbox and sales@ is its own mailbox; each syncs Inbox, Sent and read state, and an email that reached two inboxes is stored once. A reply goes from the inbox the customer wrote to; a new email from the deal's inbox (set on the deal), else hello@. A newly connected inbox imports its recent history quietly (no alerts or reminders). search_emails searches every inbox (filter `inbox`) and says which inbox each email is in.
- **Cc, Bcc and subject**: a reply goes to everyone on the thread (Cc) unless changed, and the card shows From, To, Cc, Bcc and Subject exactly as sent (the confirm code covers them). Bcc goes only with the Owner's yes, never automatically. A reply may have its own subject and still thread; a thread begun by a web form notification takes the subject of its first real email.
- **Duplicate deals**: two open deals of one contact or organization opened within crm.deal_dup.window_days (30) are checked daily and when a deal is made: the newer one is held (no automatic reminders or follow-ups) and the Owner gets a "Merge two deals" card (deal.merge; tools find_duplicate_deals, propose_deal_merge). Merging moves everything to the deal kept and closes the other as disqualified (duplicate); rejecting the card means "not one deal" and lifts the hold.
- **Promote a lead from the MCP client** (5 Nov 2026): propose_lead_promote makes the lead.promote card for an open lead (new, triaging, qualifying or nurture); confirmed, the deal starts at Qualifying with the lead's contact, organization, title and offering line. A lead that is a deal already answers with its deal. A quotation made before the deal is then linked with propose_quotation_link_deal (no revision).
