# Knowledge base, SOPs and AI Knowledge

Two libraries: the Knowledge Base for people (articles and SOPs, which also ground the AI), and AI Knowledge, documents only the AI reads (price rules, playbooks, product facts, and this ERP guide).

- **Knowledge Base** (/knowledge/articles, /knowledge/categories): articles draft, review, published, archived; types wiki, sop, runbook, guide, policy, template, faq. Publishing needs kb:publish; SOPs also need kb:manage_sops, the author's and the Owner's signatures and the Owner's approval (sop_article policy). Published SOPs are searched for every chat answer (search_sops) and cited by title.
- **AI Knowledge** (Settings → Nary & AI → AI knowledge): Markdown or PDF documents indexed for the AI only; each can be switched off, and marked customer-safe when Nary may use it with customers (never for costs, tiers, margins or internal rules). The ERP guide documents are built from the code and kept current automatically: they can be switched off, not edited.
- **How the chat uses them**: the most relevant SOP excerpts and AI Knowledge passages are added to each question; search_ai_knowledge and erp_guide look up more.
- **Outside agents (MCP)**: Any MCP client (an AI desktop app, a coding agent) can work in the ERP at /api/v1/mcp, always as one person with that person's permissions. It connects by browser login (the agent opens /oauth/authorize, the person logs in and picks the level) or with a personal token made in Settings → Integrations & access → MCP access. Levels: Read only (look things up); Propose (every change becomes an approval card, applied as that person once approved); Act as me (changes apply at once, but messages to customers, meetings and invoices stay cards); Full power (Owner), only for Nary's approver, is the Owner's one level and makes your AI client (MCP) work like Nary: open topics, decide cards, answer topics, pause Nary, the chat context, Nary's memory, a debrief as text, plus messages to customers as Nary, meetings and invoice drafts. Those, and applying a money card, go after the Owner's yes, by a second call with the one-time code the first call returned (it shows the final version; the code expires after mcp.owner.confirm_minutes and works once). Connections made earlier with Owner powers alone keep them; an agent asking for them now gets full power. A logged-in agent gets Nary's rules (and for the Owner the open topics) in the handshake, so it works like Nary without choosing a prompt. Agents can read the company's rules, this guide, the catalog (full detail only for cost viewers) and approved skills, and use the prompt Nary works with. Everything they do is recorded as coming from MCP.

- **Product files and videos in the document library** (5 Nov 2026): a catalog item's Product files are documents of the document library (Knowledge Base -> Documents, category Products, linked to the product). A version may be a PDF, an image or a video (MP4, MOV or WebM, judged by the file's content, at most nary.upload.video_max_mb; other files documents.max_file_mb); a video's length is shown and the viewer plays it. Every copy of a video that is viewed, downloaded or sent is logged with its own fingerprint id, but a video is never watermarked (the trace screen says so). On WhatsApp an MP4 up to nary.wa.video_max_mb goes as a playable video, a larger one or a MOV / WebM as a document; an email carries it up to nary.attach.max_total_mb, else as a download link. find_product_files lists library videos with their length and how they send.
- **Adding a product file from the chat or the MCP client**: the Owner asks Nary (or an MCP client) to add a file to a product, and propose_product_file makes a card: the product (id, SKU or name), the file (a CRM attachment such as a video on a deal, a file sent in the chat, an email attachment of the CRM inbox), the title, the audience (customer by default, or partner or internal), the language, and optionally an existing document to add it to as a new version. The card shows the file's type, size and a video's length and whether it is a new document or a new version; it is the Owner's only, always a card (Ask, never promoted, confirmed twice), and once confirmed the file goes live at once. Store once: a CRM attachment is not copied, the product file uses the same stored file, and deleting the attachment later keeps the file for the product; remove_from_record takes the attachment off its record after applying.
