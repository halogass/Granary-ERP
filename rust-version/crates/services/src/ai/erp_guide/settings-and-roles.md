# Settings, users and roles

Everything configurable lives in Settings, and every user's access comes from their roles' permission codes.

- **Settings areas** (/settings; a search box finds any setting): Company & branding (identity, document numbers, regional, logo, favicon, login page, letterhead, knowledge & documents), Sales & CRM (funnel, CRM attributes, follow-up reminders, quotations, quotation notes templates, pricing rules, pricing & payment terms, proposals, cost breakdown & rate card, partners, scheduled messages), Money & tax (currencies, accounting, invoices, taxes), People & payroll (attendance, office locations, kiosk tablets, payroll, overtime), Nary & AI (AI features, providers & keys, model per job, budget, chat persona, AI chat, web research, company enrichment, AI knowledge and Nary's own sections), Email & WhatsApp (email signature, system email, CRM inboxes, mail sync, email campaigns, WhatsApp link), Approvals & signing (approval policies, e-signing), Operations (projects, procurement, manufacturing, fleet & maintenance), Integrations & access (MCP access, users & roles, GitLab), System & security (login security, public pages, other settings). Users at /settings/users, GitLab at /settings/gitlab.
- **MCP Access**: each person sees and revokes their own connections (personal tokens and agents logged in by browser) and makes a token at a chosen level (Read only, Propose, Act as me; Full power (Owner) only for Nary's approver). The superadmin and system admins also see and revoke everyone's (All connections). Login lifetimes are settings under Integrations & access → MCP access: mcp.oauth.access_minutes, mcp.oauth.refresh_days, mcp.oauth.code_minutes, mcp.pat.default_days, mcp.owner.confirm_minutes (how long a full-power confirm code lasts).
- **Rule**: every business number (tax rates, social contributions, thresholds, reminder days, validity days, tolerances) is a setting; a missing one stops the feature with an error naming its key. API keys live in Settings (encrypted), never in the server environment.
- **Roles**: superadmin (everything; acts as the Owner in approvals), executive (almost everything incl. catalog, costs, finance, HR compensation, approval policies), team_lead, sales_bizdev (own CRM, quotations, discounts, manual prices), marketing, engineer, project_manager, finance_admin, hr_admin, manufacturing_tech, system_admin (settings, users, roles, audit), helpdesk_support, inventory_controller, maintenance_facilities_tech, partner_portal and vendor_portal (portal users, outside internal modules). Job titles map to default roles. Full codes: the permissions section.
- System settings permissions: system:manage_settings, system:manage_users, system:manage_roles, system:view_audit_log.

- **Two-factor sign-in (TOTP, 2 Nov 2026)**: anyone can turn it on in Profile > Security: scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password...), enter a code to confirm, then save the 10 one-time recovery codes (shown once; Download or Copy). From then on every login (the web login, the partner and vendor logins, and an agent's MCP browser login, which goes through the web login) asks for the 6-digit code after the password, or "Use a recovery code" (each works once). Turning it off needs a code; new recovery codes need a code. Creating an MCP token that can act asks the password and the code. Lost phone and no recovery codes: the Owner or a system admin uses "Reset two-factor" on the user in Settings > Users (audit-logged) and the person sets it up again. People with manufacturing:manage_production must have it on to approve device pairings (each approval asks a fresh code). Settings under System & security → Login security: auth.totp.issuer (the name the app shows), auth.totp.challenge_seconds, auth.totp.max_failed_attempts and auth.totp.failed_window_seconds (wrong codes per person), auth.totp.recovery_codes. Wrong codes also count toward the login limits.
