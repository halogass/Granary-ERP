# Manufacturing and inventory: BOMs, production, units, QC, warranty, stock

Manufacturing builds the company's hardware: bills of materials, production orders, a registry of every unit with its QC, warranty and RMA, and the stock it uses.

- **BOMs** (/manufacturing/products): draft, approved, archived (manufacturing:manage_bom). A new BOM goes to approval at once: the Manufacturing Lead, and the Owner too when one unit costs more than the policy's limit (Settings → Approvals & signing → Approval policies). Approving it archives the product's previous BOM; production uses the approved one.
- **Production orders** (/manufacturing/production, manufacturing:create_production_order to create, manufacturing:manage_production to move): planned → component_check → in_production → qc → completed (or back a step, or cancelled). Going into production needs a BOM and reserves the components (refused when stock is short); going back or cancelling releases them; completing adds the finished goods.
- **Units** (/manufacturing/units): in_production, qc_pending, qc_passed, qc_failed, rework, in_stock, reserved, shipped, returned.
- **QC** (/manufacturing/qc, manufacturing:log_qc): pass → qc_passed; fail → qc_failed; conditional → qc_passed with its conditions (required, in the notes).
- **Warranty and RMA** (/manufacturing/warranty, manufacturing:manage_warranty): warranty active, expired, rma_open, rma_closed, voided; opening an RMA creates a case (open, received, diagnosing, repair, replacement, closed, rejected).
- **Stock** (/manufacturing/inventory, /manufacturing/warehouses, /manufacturing/transfers): components with adjustments (manufacturing:manage_inventory); warehouses with zones, aisles, racks and bins; stock moves (receipt, issue, internal, adjustment) and transfers (inventory:view / inventory:manage). Goods receipts from purchase orders add stock.

- **Generated unit serials (3 Nov 2026, Owner)**: "Register unit" does not ask for a serial: the ERP generates it, PREFIX-XXXXX-XXXXC (e.g. LPT-7K2M9-QXH48), shown with a copy button and printed on the label (whose QR opens the unit by its serial). PREFIX is the hardware product's Serial prefix (2 to 6 letters, set on Manufacturing > Products: LPT for the HW-LPT products, MON for HW-MON); a product without one is refused with a clear message (no default). A test unit (the "Test unit" box when adding it, a Test pill on the unit) gets the prefix plus T (LPTT-..., MONT-...). The 9 characters are Crockford base32 of 45 random bits of the unit's UUID; the last is a check symbol, so a mistyped generated serial is refused with "This serial's check character does not match: probably a typo". Typed or searched serials (the unit search, the label link, warranty) ignore case, dashes and spaces and read I and L as 1, O as 0; old free-form serials (LPT-TEST-000001) work unchanged. Each normalised serial belongs to one unit. A unit can be relabelled any time ("Edit serial" on the unit's device panel): refused when another unit has that serial; for a paired unit the License Server keeps the serial it was paired with, so print a new label. A box claimed again after a reset (License Server box.claimed with a previous claim) is noted on the unit and the Owner is alerted, even when the unit is shipped. Setting: manufacturing.serial.generate_tries.
