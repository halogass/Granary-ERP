# Code of conduct

Granary is built by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience. We want taking part
to be a good experience for everyone.

## What we expect
- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, never with people.
- Be patient with newcomers; everyone started somewhere.
- Keep feedback specific and constructive, in issues, pull requests and discussions alike.
- Respect people's time: search before asking, and give enough detail for others to help.

## What is not acceptable
- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background or ability.
- Sexualised language or imagery, threats, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may block people who
repeatedly or seriously break it. Report a problem privately: use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment or
issue, or open a private report under **Security > Report a vulnerability** and say it is a conduct report. Reports
are kept confidential.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct issues: report them as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
